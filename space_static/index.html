GENCO Operator Console

This console shows GENCO, the GridFM grid foundation model, on IEEE 118 contingencies. After each Newton step, a calibrated bound on every monitored branch loading and PQ voltage decides whether the screening decision can be accepted. Otherwise the policy takes another step, and after three steps it falls back to a full solve. The same policy runs from the pre-contingency state for comparison. All safety evidence is empirical.
